Password Policy (SOP)
  • Must be minimum 12 characters with uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and special symbols (e.g., !, @, #, $).
  • Mandatory rotation every 90 days via automated system prompt.
  • Never share credentials with anyone. IT personnel will never request your password.
Physical Security (SOP)
  • Always lock your terminal (Windows Key + L) whenever stepping away from your workspace.
  • Do not attach personal flash drives, external HDDs, or mobile devices without written IT authorization.
Phishing Awareness (SOP)
  • Do not click unexpected links or download unverified attachments from unfamiliar senders.
  • Inspect sender headers carefully for spoofed domains (e.g. @pbl-support.com instead of official PBL).
  • Forward suspected phishing emails directly to the IT Department for domain isolation.
Web Usage Policy (SOP)
  • Bandwidth is reserved exclusively for business and client production workloads.
  • Accessing unauthorized streaming platforms, torrents, or gaming servers triggers security alerts.
Data Storage & Backup (SOP)
  • Store all operational files on company Server Shares or OneDrive Cloud ONLY.
  • Local Desktop or C: drive data is NOT backed up and will be unrecoverable during disk failure.
Incident Response (SOP)
  • Immediately report lost or stolen hardware (laptops, security dongles) to IT Management.
  • If ransomware or malware is suspected, unplug LAN cable immediately and report in person.

PBL maintains an uncompromised Zero Tolerance Policy for any form of harassment, discrimination, or verbal misconduct in the office environment.

  • Employees can report grievances without apprehension of retaliation directly to HR or POSH committee members.
  • Every complaint is logged under strict non-disclosure protocols and investigated by an internal committee.
