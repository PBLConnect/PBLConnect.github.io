PBL Leave Policy Guidelines

OFFICIAL SOP

Leave entitlements, carry-over limits, emergency protocols, and instant balance checker.

Casual Leave (CL): 7 Days/Yr
Sick Leave (SL): 7 Days/Yr
Paid Leave (PL): 18 Days/Yr
Max PL Cap: 45 Days
Policy Clauses & FAQs Click to expand

Every confirmed employee is entitled to the following leaves per calendar year (Apr 1st to Mar 31st):

  • Casual Leave (CL): 7 Days for unforeseen personal reasons or short, planned breaks.
  • Sick Leave (SL): 7 Days for personal illness. Medical certificate required for 3+ consecutive days.
  • Paid Leave (PL): 18 Days for planned vacations. Credited at 1.5 days per completed month.
  • Short Breaks (1-3 Days): Submit intranet leave form at least 3 working days in advance.
  • Long Vacations (> 3 Days): Formal notice of 15 days is mandatory for resource alignment.
  • Sick Leave Notice: Inform reporting manager & HR via call/email on Day 1 before 10:00 AM.
  • Maternity Benefit: 26 weeks paid leave for female colleagues as per statutory regulations.
  • Paternity Leave: 5 days paid leave for confirmed male staff within 1 month of delivery.
  • Leave Without Pay (LWP): Subject to management approval only after all balances lapse.
  • CL & SL Lapsing: Unused Casual & Sick leaves lapse on 31st March (no carry forward or cash-out).
  • PL Carryover: A maximum cap of 45 PL days can roll over into next financial year.
  • PL Cashout: Leaves accumulating beyond 45 days are encashed as per payroll formula.
