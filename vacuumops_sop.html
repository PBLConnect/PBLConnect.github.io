System Overview 🎯

PBL VacuumOps ek highly advanced, secure aur automated IT Asset Maintenance portal hai. Iska primary objective hai company ke saare workstations, servers, aur critical hardware ki timely physical cleaning (vacuuming) ko track karna.

Regular cleaning se computers overheating se bachte hain, unki processing speed maintain rehti hai, aur hardware failures drastically kam hote hain. Ye SOP aapko is portal ko efficiently use karne ka sahi tarika batayegi.

The 7-Day Grace Period

Agar aap upar diye gaye main mahine (e.g., Jan, May, Sept) mein apna task 100% complete nahi kar paate hain, toh VacuumOps system aapko 7 Days ka Extra Buffer deta hai.

  • Example: May ke cycle ka grace period 1st June se 7th June tak chalega.
  • In 7 dino mein Dashboard ke top par ek Amber Alert Banner lagatar dikhega jo countdown dikhayega.
  • CRITICAL RULE: 8th day aate hi system automatically aur forcefully LOCK ho jayega. Uske baad application Disable ho jayegi aur purani pending entries ko agle 4 mahine tak update nahi kiya ja sakega!

Location & Filter Setup

App kholte hi sabse pehle header dropdown se apni Location (Noida / Dehradun) chunein. Iske baad, Left panel (ya mobile mein cards ke upar) diye gaye filters mein se "Pending" par click karein. Isse aapko wahi PCs dikhenge jo abhi saaf nahi huye hain.

Reporting & Email Triggers 📧

Mid-Cycle Report Button

Agar Head Office se progress mangi jaye, toh bottom right mein diye gaye "Report" button (Jahaj wale icon) ko dabayein. Ye current status (Kitne Done, Kitne Pending) ka ek khubsurat graphical email automatically IT Head ko bhej dega.

100% Completion Closure

Jaise hi aapki branch ke saare (100%) PCs saaf ho jayenge aur koi 'Pending' nahi bachega, screen par ek bada Complete 🎉 pop-up fat-te huye aayega. Usme Final Send button dabane par Cycle close ho jayegi.

The Golden Rules

Use the Sync Button

Agar Noida aur Dehradun ek sath kaam kar rahe hain, toh bottom menu mein diye gaye ghoomne wale icon ka use karein, page refresh kiye bina naya data fetch ho jayega.

Dark Mode & Search

Top right corner mein 🌙 moon icon se Dark Mode on karein (Battery aur aankhon ke liye accha). Aur Magnifying glass se kis bhi system ka Tag daalkar directly usko dhundein.

NEVER Alter Asset Tags

Asset Tag hamesha company format mein hona chahiye (e.g., PBL-NOD-IT-CPU-001). Agar tag format galat hoga toh Inventory Master sheet usko reject kar degi.
